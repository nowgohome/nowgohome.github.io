🐾 Animal Shorts

Three real animal facts in under 30 seconds, every day.

What this is

Animal Shorts is a personal YouTube Shorts channel. A small private tool, run by the channel owner, puts together one short video per day: licensed stock footage of a real animal, an English voice-over with three facts, and English and Spanish subtitles.

How the app uses YouTube

The tool uses the YouTube Data API only to upload these videos and their English and Spanish caption tracks to the owner's own channel. It is not offered to the public and does not access anyone else's account. See the Privacy Policy for details.
